## 🚀 How to Switch Between Pages

### Current Setup
Each page is accessible via its route:
- Main: `http://localhost:3000/`
- Test: `http://localhost:3000/test`
- Test2: `http://localhost:3000/test2`
- Premium: `http://localhost:3000/premium`

### To Make Premium the Main Page
Replace content in `app/page.tsx` with content from `app/premium/page.tsx`

### To Add Navigation
Add links in the header to switch between pages during development
